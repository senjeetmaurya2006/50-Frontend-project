How it works

The physics of liquid form

Organic merge

Blobs find each other through proximity and fuse into unified forms, then split again — emergent behavior from simple physics with no hard-coded interactions.

Color fusion

Where blobs overlap, their pigments mix in the frequency domain — not additive blending, but true spectral synthesis produced by gaussian blur before threshold.

Perpetual motion

Each body follows its own elliptical orbit at its own cadence. The system never repeats — no two seconds look alike, no pattern ever resolves.
